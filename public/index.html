What Actually Moves This Stock?
Earnings, news & macro-event impact vs. an ordinary day’s movement
Enter a ticker (e.g. MU, TSLA), commodity (gold, silver), or sector name (technology, energy) and click Analyze.
The full analysis pulls ~2 years of prices plus earnings, news and Fed/CPI/jobs release dates, so it can take 30–60 seconds.
Computed on demand by stock_movers.py --json <ticker> — nothing is written to disk; recent results are cached by the server for 10 minutes and by the page for the session.
