AAPL — what actually moves it

Price range 2024-09-24 .. 2026-09-23 (2y) · report generated 2026-09-24 10:31 AEST · 122 events collected

In plain English

  • Earnings reports tend to move AAPL's price noticeably more than a typical day — about 2.5% the day after, versus about 1.1% on an ordinary day.
  • CPI inflation reports, Fed rate decisions, and jobs reports did not move AAPL's price meaningfully differently than an ordinary day — the stock's normal day-to-day ups and downs already cover about as much ground.
  • There isn't enough news headlines data yet to say anything reliable about how they move AAPL's price.

For context: a typical day for AAPL moves the price about 0.7% (median), while the average skip day is 1.1% -- the average is pulled up by a few extreme days, so the median is the better 'usual day' reference when judging these reactions.

That's historical pattern-matching over a limited sample, not a prediction of future moves.

Data notes

  • Prices: Yahoo Finance daily closes over the 2-year window, auto_adjust=True (split/dividend-adjusted); any missing day renders as a gap in the chart line.
  • Events: Yahoo earnings dates, Yahoo Finance RSS headlines, and FRED macro releases (FEDFUNDS, CPIAUCSL, PAYEMS).
  • Control pool: 220 ordinary trading days (each event's day, the 2 days before it, and the 3 days after it are excluded); next-day avg |move| 1.06% (median 0.71%, sd 0.99%), 3-day avg |move| 2.10% (median 1.66%).
  • Realized volatility 28.7% annualized (std of daily log returns x sqrt(252)) implies a typical daily move of about 1.44%, which is consistent with the measured ordinary-day baseline of 1.06%.
  • Legend: r1 = return from event-day close to next trading-day close; r3 = return over the following 3 trading days; 'control' = same-size random sample of ordinary days; 'mdn |r|' = median absolute return (the 'middle' move, less inflated by extreme days than the mean).
  • 17 event(s) fell outside the measurable price window (before it, in the future, or without +3 trading days of history yet, e.g. today's headlines); they stay in the events CSV with blank returns so nothing is silently dropped.
  • Full per-event log with every return: aapl_events.csv (in the same folder, for spreadsheet use).
